## Users

My Concierge SA serves four client types, all confirmed as in-scope (the founder does
not want the site to exclude any of them):

- **Inbound international travellers** — high-net-worth visitors coming to Cape Town
  (and wider South Africa) for leisure who want trips, restaurant tables, safaris,
  winelands, yacht and helicopter charters arranged before and during their stay.
- **Affluent Cape Town locals** — residents who outsource restaurant and event
  bookings, personal errands, special-occasion planning and ongoing lifestyle admin.
- **Executives & corporate** — business travellers, personal assistants booking on
  behalf of a principal, corporate hospitality, and close-protection coordination.
- **Relocations / expats** — people moving to or spending extended time in Cape Town
  who need setup, logistics and local navigation.

The common thread: the client has money and little time, values discretion and
reliability, and wants one trusted person to handle things rather than an app or a
call centre.

## Product Purpose

A private concierge and lifestyle-management service based in Cape Town, delivered
personally by its founder. It exists to save clients time, reduce friction and
stress, and give them access to experiences, reservations and introductions they
could not easily arrange themselves. Success is a client who hands over a request —
one booking or an entire trip — and trusts it will be handled without follow-up.

## Operating Context

- First contact is by web enquiry form, WhatsApp, phone or email; WhatsApp is
  positioned as the fastest route and is expected to carry a large share of
  real conversations.
- The business operates "by appointment" with flexible hours; it is a solo
  operation, so responsiveness and personal reply are part of the promise.
- Services are grouped as: **Travel & Transport**, **Lifestyle & Personal**, and
  **Bespoke & Exclusive**; plus a set of Cape Town / South Africa "experiences"
  (winelands & private cellars, ocean & marine, helicopter & air charters, safari
  & wildlife, city & cultural access).
- Languages: English and Afrikaans.
- Service area: Cape Town primarily; clients served locally and internationally,
  with arrangements extending South Africa-wide (e.g. Kruger safaris) and beyond.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- **Engagement model:** both per-request / per-booking (one-offs, travellers) and
  an ongoing retainer / membership option (locals wanting continuous lifestyle
  management). Specific fees, tiers and retainer pricing are **not yet defined**
  and must not be invented on the site.
- Single-operator business — capacity is finite; the site should not imply a large
  team or 24/7 call-centre coverage.
- Some services (close protection, private jet, diamond/jewellery access) are
  coordinated through third-party providers, not delivered in-house.
- Current implementation: a single static `index.html` (inline CSS/JS), contact
  form posts to formsubmit.co, hosted at myconciergesa.com. No build system, no
  framework, no CMS.
- Domain and name **My Concierge SA** are established and in use.

## Product Principles

1. **One person, personally.** Every touchpoint should reinforce that a named,
   experienced individual handles the request — never dilute this into agency
   language.
2. **Discretion outranks spectacle.** The client base values privacy; proof and
   confidence should be shown quietly, not shouted.
3. **Serve all four client types without fragmenting.** The offer is broad by
   design; the site must let a traveller, a local, an executive and a relocating
   family each see themselves without splitting into separate products.
4. **Make first contact effortless.** Enquiry, WhatsApp, phone and email must all
   be obvious and low-friction; the decision to reach out is the only conversion
   that matters.
5. **Never over-claim.** No invented pricing, testimonials, team size, or
   availability promises the solo operation cannot keep.
