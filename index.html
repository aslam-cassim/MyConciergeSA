About

A career built on exceptional service

My name is Sedick Achmat, and hospitality has been my passion and profession for more than 35 years. I have had the privilege of serving guests in some of the finest five-star hotels, where I learned that true concierge service is about much more than providing information - it's about creating exceptional experiences, solving problems, and making life easier for people.

Over the years I have assisted countless guests from around the world with everything from travel arrangements and exclusive reservations to personalised recommendations and bespoke requests. These experiences gave me a deep understanding of what clients value most: reliability, discretion, attention to detail, and genuine care.

Today I bring that same standard to My Concierge SA - a service designed to save you time, reduce stress, and help you enjoy life's opportunities with confidence and convenience.
